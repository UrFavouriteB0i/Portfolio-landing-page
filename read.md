## 🔍 Part 4: Critical Questions to Ask HR (Establishing Authority)

When HR asks *"Do you have any questions for me?"*, asking basic questions makes you look passive. Instead, ask these **strategic, probing questions** to demonstrate that you evaluate opportunities critically:

### Category 1: Client Engagement & Operational Realities
1. **On Role Composition:**
   > *"In this Client Serving role, what is the typical ratio between **long-term client implementation embedded teams** versus **short-term discovery, RFP architecture, and Proof-of-Concept (PoC) builds**?"*
2. **On Client AI Maturity in Indonesia:**
   > *"When Technology Consulting engages enterprise clients in Jakarta today, are most engagements focused on building AI foundational platforms (like data pipelines & RAG), or are clients actively demanding autonomous multi-agent systems in production?"*

### Category 2: Technical Autonomy vs. Enterprise Constraints
3. **On Stack Governance:**
   > *"EY has strong global alliances with Microsoft Azure and Databricks. When designing agentic solutions, how much autonomy does the engineering team have to introduce custom open-source frameworks (e.g., FastAPI, LangGraph, custom MCP protocols) alongside proprietary cloud vendor platforms?"*

### Category 3: Team Structure & Performance Metrics
4. **On Team Topology:**
   > *"How is a typical Tech Consulting AI delivery squad structured? Will I be working alongside dedicated Cloud Architects, Data Engineers, and Scrum Masters, or are AI Consultants expected to own the end-to-end DevOps pipeline independently?"*
5. **On Work Dynamics & Logistics:**
   > *"Given the hybrid nature of consulting in Jakarta, what is the current expected balance between working from the EY office (World Trade Centre), client site presence, and remote work for the Technology Consulting practice?"*
